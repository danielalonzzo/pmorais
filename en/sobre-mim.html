Osteopath and Personal Training Specialist

Paulo Morais is a specialist in personal training, with over 20 years of experience. After a professional journey in gyms, schools, sports clubs, and physiotherapy clinics, he developed his unique methodology in a one-on-one process. Today, he guides each client with an exclusive plan tailored to their needs, pathologies, and lifestyle.

Whether the goal is to improve posture, lose weight, recover from surgery, or run a marathon, for example. He is one of the pioneers in Portugal in specialised training for oncology patients, a knowledge area where he continues to invest. Another feature that sets him apart as a personal trainer is the fact that he is an osteopath, which greatly expands the strategies he can apply in each training session.

Personal training in Lisbon

Personal training with Paulo Morais provides individual coaching adapted to goals, physical condition and lifestyle. The in-person service is based in Lisbon, Portugal.

Private training focuses on one person through a programme suited to individual needs. It forms part of a wider offer covering group training, online coaching, osteopathy and cancer exercise.

Individual coaching with defined goals

Choosing private training depends on the goal, previous experience and preferred level of support. A programme can address different priorities without assuming identical outcomes for every person.

Fitness and strength

A training goal may involve developing physical fitness and strength. Exercise selection and progression should reflect the individual starting point.

Routine and continuity

The programme considers lifestyle and available time for training. The organisation of coaching aims to make practice compatible with daily routines.

Specific goals

Paulo Morais has worked with goals such as improving posture or preparing for a marathon. Each goal requires an approach suited to the individual context.

Preparing for personal training

The initial contact helps clarify the goal and appropriate training format. Programme planning should consider previous training experience and needs relevant to exercise.

Information before starting

Is personal training an individual service?

This format provides individual coaching, also known as private training or one-to-one personal training. Group training is a separate option described on its own page.

Is previous training experience required?

Previous experience helps define the starting point. The initial contact allows goals and needs to be discussed before choosing a coaching format.

Is coaching available outside Lisbon?

In-person coaching is based in Lisbon. Online training allows remote enquiries from Portugal and the international markets covered by the service.

How are prices and availability confirmed?

Prices, availability and coaching conditions are clarified directly during contact. An enquiry can be sent by WhatsApp or email, identifying the preferred training format.
