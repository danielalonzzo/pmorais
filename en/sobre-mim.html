About Me

Paulo Morais

Paulo works with people of all ages and goals: improving posture, losing weight, recovering from surgery, preparing for a marathon, or simply living with more energy. He is one of the first in Portugal to develop training for oncology patients.

He loves the sea, his family, and the adrenaline of a new challenge. He loves to travel, whether it's hiking, a jeep tour, or a motorcycle trip.

"Paulo is a people person, always ready for a good chat."

Paulo Dimas Morais

Paulo Morais is an osteopathy technician, maintaining the mindset of treating each client as unique, with integrated and personalised follow-up. In addition to paying attention to pathology and age group, he is careful to align with each person's lifestyle, so that the sessions are effective.

Osteopath and Personal Training Specialist

Paulo Morais is a specialist in personal training, with over 20 years of experience. After a professional journey in gyms, schools, sports clubs, and physiotherapy clinics, he developed his unique methodology in a one-on-one process. Today, he guides each client with an exclusive plan tailored to their needs, pathologies, and lifestyle.

Whether the goal is to improve posture, lose weight, recover from surgery, or run a marathon, for example. He is one of the pioneers in Portugal in specialised training for oncology patients, a knowledge area where he continues to invest. Another feature that sets him apart as a personal trainer is the fact that he is an osteopath, which greatly expands the strategies he can apply in each training session.

Step 2 · Look after your mind after cancer

How are you,
really after cancer?

Cancer treatment ends, but your mind does not switch off straight away. The fear of the cancer coming back, sleep, sadness and the tiredness of «having to be strong» can last for years. Nobody is marking you here: flip the stones and see if any of them speaks to you.

InhaleExhale

When cancer treatment ends, many people feel left alone: the team that saw them every week suddenly disappears. Feeling that is normal, and there is help.

This is not a test or a diagnosis, and nothing is saved or sent. If it weighs on you, talk to your healthcare team or a psycho-oncology professional. In a crisis, call 112 or SNS 24 (808 24 24 24, Portugal).
