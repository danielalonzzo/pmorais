Step 3 · Protect yourself after cancer

Your shield after cancer
has 4 pieces

After cancer treatment, your defences can take time to recover. Vaccines against flu, COVID-19, pneumococcal disease and shingles are among the most important. Tick the ones you have already confirmed with your team and watch the shield close.

Already confirmed with my doctor:

Tap a vaccineSee why it matters and tick it once you have confirmed it with your doctor.

Flu

A Cochrane review in immunosuppressed adults with cancer concluded that flu vaccination is safe, and observational data point to lower mortality and better infection-related outcomes.

Ask your doctor«When should I have the flu vaccine, and which one?»

COVID-19

ASCO recommends systematically checking the vaccination status of people who have had cancer, including COVID-19, as part of follow-up care.

Ask your doctor«Which COVID-19 schedule is right for me?»

Pneumococcal

Pneumococcal disease can cause serious infections such as pneumonia. It is among the vaccines ASCO includes in the review after cancer.

Ask your doctor«Am I up to date with the pneumococcal vaccine?»

Shingles

Shingles (herpes zoster) is one of the infections to watch for when defences are low. The vaccine is also part of the recommended review.

Ask your doctor«Is the shingles vaccine suitable for me, and when?»

Shield complete! Keep reviewing your vaccination record with your doctor; your defences and the recommendations change over time.

Take this to your appointment

Your doctor decides which vaccines you need: Paulo does not prescribe or recommend vaccines. General information based on ASCO guidance; it does not replace your oncology team.
