# Online training in Portugal and abroad

Online training with Paulo Morais allows remote coaching enquiries from his base in Lisbon. Programme planning considers goals, individual needs and the training setting.

The service covers Portugal, the European Union, and Portuguese-, English- and Spanish-speaking countries. The remote format, availability and coaching conditions are clarified directly during contact.

Online coaching is one of Paulo Morais’s training formats. The wider offer also includes private personal training, group training, osteopathy and cancer exercise.

Online availability refers to exercise coaching rather than a hands-on osteopathy appointment. Enquiries about adapted exercise during or after cancer should be discussed within the oncology training service.
